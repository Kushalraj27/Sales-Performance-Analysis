**SALES PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS | POWER BI**

**1.Project Overview**

This project analyzes global sales performance using Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into revenue, profit, sales trends, regional performance, product categories, and order priorities to support data-driven business decisions.

---

**2.Business Problem**

Organizations need a centralized reporting solution to monitor sales performance, identify profitable regions and products, and track revenue trends over time.

This dashboard was developed to:

-> Monitor key business KPIs
-> Identify high-performing regions
-> Analyze product profitability
-> Track yearly revenue trends
-> Compare sales channels
-> Support strategic decision-making

**6.Key Insights**

### Regional Performance

* Sub-Saharan Africa generated the highest revenue (344.27M).
* Europe was the second-largest contributor.

### Product Performance

* Cosmetics generated the highest profit contribution (22.59M).

### Revenue Trends

* Revenue peaked around 2015.
* Significant decline was observed after 2015.

### Sales Channels

* Revenue contribution was evenly distributed between Online and Offline channels.

### Order Priority

* High-priority orders generated the highest profit.

---

**7.Business Recommendations**

1. Expand operations in high-performing regions such as Sub-Saharan Africa and Europe.
2. Increase investment in Cosmetics and Household categories.
3. Investigate factors causing the revenue decline after 2015.
4. Optimize low-performing regions through targeted marketing campaigns.
5. Improve customer acquisition strategies in countries with lower revenue generation.
